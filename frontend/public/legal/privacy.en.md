# Privacy Policy

This page explains how `{{institution_name}}` uses personal data. The text below is the draft structure (docs/legal/privacy-and-consent.md §8.1) - replace this file with the text reviewed by a lawyer.

## 1. Who we are
`{{institution_name}}` is the data controller; the software provider `{{platform_name}}` is the processor. Contact: `{{contact}}`.

## 2. What we keep
Student name, date of birth, photo; guardian phone; attendance, results, fees; (where used) health and behaviour.

## 3. Why we keep it
Admission and running the classes; fee collection; messages to guardians; legal duties. Never for advertising or sale.

## 4. Who we share it with
SMS and payment providers (only what they need); teachers see only their own students.

## 5. How long we keep it
- SMS log: 180 days (the message text is removed after 90 days)
- Audit log: 2 years
- Payment receipts: as financial records, as the law requires
- When an institution closes: everything is removed in about 60 days

## 6. Your rights
See (download the data), correct, stop SMS, withdraw consent, ask for erasure (through the institution; records the law requires are kept).

## 7. Security
Encrypted connections, access control, logs.

## 8. Children
Data of students under 18 is kept with a guardian's consent.

## 9. Changes
Dated versions; guardians are told about major changes.

## 10. Complaints
First the institution, then `{{platform_contact}}`.
