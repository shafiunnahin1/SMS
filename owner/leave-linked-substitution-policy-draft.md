## P1-P15 policy clauses - owner finalized; implementation gated

P1. Occurrences and optional coverage. Build slots from an actual source routine and institution-local date within the accepted leave working-date set; omit non-teaching occurrences. Do not fabricate routine periods for an institution/type without routines. Display every affected slot even without a nominee. A gap never automatically rejects leave. For a pending application, proposals reflect its current valid dates; after approval use the accepted D85 snapshot, not a later calendar reinterpretation. Schedule/version changes trigger reconciliation rather than silent historical mutation.

P2. Advisory nomination. The applicant may nominate an eligible teacher only for their own pending leave, before the period starts. A nomination does not reserve time, notify a teacher to accept, authorize attendance, or become an offer automatically. Recheck date/full interval and identity on save. Invalid nomination is rejected without discarding unrelated valid leave data; the applicant may submit an unassigned slot. Pending leave/nomination does not block other applicants' available-teacher lists as a confirmed booking would.

P3. Review and offer. Only an authorized reviewer can approve leave and keep/replace/clear a nominee, or fill an approved leave's future gap. A reviewed nominee becomes an offer only for an approved leave. No valid nominee or a newly conflicting nominee leaves a gap, with explicit reviewer-visible reason. Do not auto-confirm an offer. Recheck availability during approval and assignment even if the picker previously showed the teacher.

P4. Acceptance and reservations. Neither nominations nor outstanding offers reserve a booking. The target teacher alone accepts their current versioned offer, before the period starts, while leave/source/identity remain valid. Inside the shared transaction recheck all conflicts, then confirm once. Two offers may therefore exist for overlapping time; only one can become confirmed. A conflicting acceptance returns a conflict result, leaves no booking, and exposes the gap for reviewer repair. Offering UI must clearly say availability is provisional. No automatic expiry scheduler is required for the first scope; offers cease being actionable at period start or invalidation.

P5. Decline, replacement and cutoff. The target may decline an unconfirmed current offer with an optional bounded reason; slot becomes unassigned. Reviewer may clear/reassign only future occurrences, with reason after prior confirmation. Replacing confirmed cover atomically revokes old attendance authority, records supersession and creates a new unconfirmed offer; the replacement must accept. No self-service withdrawal of confirmed cover or edits at/after period start in the first scope. Exceptional current/past corrections remain a separate policy, never a hidden bypass. No hard deletion of leave, slot or offer history.

P6. Reviewer identity and conflicts. A label such as HR is not an authorization role. Applicant, reviewer and substitute operations need separately reviewed exact capability mappings, active tenant/context/membership and ownership under the prevailing staff contract. Do not invent a global role or reuse unrelated index/show keys. A reviewer can be the substitute only if they have a real eligible unambiguous active Teacher identity, are not the leave applicant, and accept in that identity. Proposed conservative rule: applicant cannot approve their own leave or review their own nomination; no implicit sole-admin exception for this new workflow. Owner must approve how this interacts with existing principal leave powers.

P7. Date changes and cancellation. First scope keeps dates immutable on an existing submitted application; change dates through D85-permitted cancel/reapply. No new approved/current/past amendment endpoint. Pending reject/cancel cancels its proposals. Before-start approved cancellation atomically cancels future offers/covers with audit. Replacement application creates new linked slots, never retargets old accepted offers. Changes which D85 disallows remain denied; do not claim general date-amendment support. Past accepted attendance and financial snapshots remain unchanged.

P8. Other teacher leave and source changes. If a confirmed substitute later gets approved leave, the same transaction must invalidate their affected future covers into unassigned slots, record the reason and notify the original leave's reviewer; it must not cancel another person's leave. A source routine teacher/time/batch change or deletion must reconcile affected future slots under the same lock, cancel stale offers/confirmation and require fresh review/acceptance. No retargeting an accepted version. Once a period starts, destructive source edits affecting it are denied in this first scope; preserve source/history links. Owner must explicitly approve these restrictions on routine writers.

P9. Eligibility and overlap. Full interval uses start < other.end AND end > other.start, so end=start is allowed. Require same tenant, real active Teacher, existing subject/batch eligibility where the approved academic contract requires it, no approved leave for that date, no overlapping recurring teaching or confirmed cover, and applicable block/class/room validity. Teacher employee/department text is not identity or permission proof. Availability is date-aware; confirmed cover overlays the source occurrence, not a second class/room booking against that very source. Check other conflicting occurrences and exclude only the same source occurrence/current assignment. No force override for confirmed cover. The original absent teacher and caller-supplied fake source IDs are never candidates.

P10. Atomicity and concurrency. Every relevant leave approval/cancel, cover assign/review/accept/replace, and routine/block writer must lock the bound institution row first in a DB transaction, as existing LeaveService does. Then lock involved existing leave/slot/offer/source rows in a documented deterministic order and re-read current versions/eligibility. Tenant-wide serialization is the proposed initial correctness tradeoff; no separate distributed lock or new lock table. Races serialize even for different routine IDs. After bounded deadlock retries, return a retryable conflict without partial effects. Uniqueness protects occurrence identity only; locking plus revalidation protects overlapping teacher time. Implementation must audit every writer, including scheduled/admin paths; a single non-participating writer keeps concurrency acceptance blocked.

P11. Logical persistence proposal, not approved DDL. Represent one stable current coverage slot per tenant/source routine/date, linked to the source leave and versioned occurrence snapshot; it may be unassigned. Keep each nomination/review/offer/accept/decline/supersession as retained versioned history with actor/time/reason. A current assignment can reference one accepted offer; changing an assignee creates a new version, never overwrites who previously accepted. Reuse/refine existing substitutions as the current slot if the reviewed schema supports it; a related history structure is a candidate, not permission to create a table. Preserve existing non-leave substitution reasons/records; no automatic conversion. Exact physical columns, nullability, tenant-safe keys/FKs, version uniqueness, purge/export and migrations must be separately specified and approved before DDL. No database probing in this planning work.

P12. Transport and actions proposal. Reuse existing documented leave/substitution/availability endpoints where feasible; do not register guessed routes. The reviewed contract must separately distinguish: own leave nominations, reviewer approval/offer/clear/replace, own target acceptance/decline and date-aware available teachers. Inputs must bind leave/source/date/expected version; the server derives actor/original teacher/tenant. Reject unknown actions and stale versions. Proposed responses: authorization403, foreign/non-owned resource404 after prerequisites, invalid data422, stale/transition/availability conflict409; exact existing error envelope and idempotency rules require canonical review. A repeated identical successful transition returns its original result; an old acceptance can never confirm a replacement. Exact paths, payloads and capability strings are intentionally not finalized here.

P13. Read projection and attendance. Only confirmed current cover for a source routine/date grants that substitute the occurrence in their dated routine and period-attendance write boundary. Pending nomination, review and offer grant nothing. Cancel/replacement removes future authority immediately; preserve attendance already taken, with actor/history, rather than rewriting taken_by. Any correction uses an independently authorized attendance correction flow. Weekly routine rows remain unchanged by every cover action. Non-leave routine CRUD changes are not treated as cover actions.

P14. Audit, notification and privacy. Transactionally retain transition audit with IDs/version/date/actor and a durable delivery intent before commit. Deliver in-app after commit, SMS only under consent/provider policy, idempotently per transition/recipient/channel. Delivery failure retries without duplicate assignment/attendance authority. Reuse existing reliable delivery primitives if suitable; a new outbox/storage component is not pre-approved. Offers disclose class/period/time and required minimal names, never medical/leave reason, salary or broad directory/contact data. Cancellation/replacement informs the affected target and authorized reviewer; no real messages during implementation tests.

P15. Payroll and rollout. All cover transitions leave payroll/expense/salary adjustment/payment rows unchanged. A future extra-class summary is informational only and outside this first scope unless explicitly approved. Preserve D87 unavailable-substitution behavior until a later approved integration exists; no fabricated payout. Routine and leave dependencies, period-attendance authorization integration, supported institution types, module gates, upgrade/backfill/rollback and exact runtime task/release scope all need acceptance before exposure. UI remains gated by the current approved plan; this draft enables no module or route.

## Proposed behavioral states (logical labels, not schema enums)

| Current slot | Allowed next event | Result and guard |
|---|---|---|
| Unassigned, pending leave | Applicant nominates | Nominated, advisory only; own pending application and valid eligibility |
| Nominated, pending leave | Applicant clears/replaces | New nomination version or unassigned; before start |
| Unassigned/nominated | Reviewer approves leave | Approved gap or reviewed offer; no automatic confirmation |
| Approved gap | Reviewer assigns | Current offered version after fresh checks |
| Offered | Target accepts | Confirmed only after locked checks; conflict leaves no confirmed booking |
| Offered | Target declines | Unassigned plus retained declined history |
| Offered/confirmed future slot | Reviewer replaces/clears | Old version superseded/revoked, gap or new unconfirmed offer |
| Any actionable future slot | Valid leave cancellation/rejection or source invalidation | Cancelled/invalidation history; never delete or silently reuse accepted version |
| At/after start | Offer acceptance, replacement, source-destroying correction | Denied in first scope; historical attendance unchanged |
